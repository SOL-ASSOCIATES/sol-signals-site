Method and sources

DEMO edition | Build 216630799f1e | 2026-10-27 17:28 UTC

Explore Romania's mapped power plants, substations and grid, and county totals from the published list of grid connection approvals (ATR).

This page describes the layers of this edition of the Romanian Energy Grid Atlas: where each one comes from, under which licence, as of which date, how it was made, and what it does not show. The map is general information, not advice.

This is a demo edition, built from open sources. All numbers are as of the build date shown above and of the date of each layer; the demo is updated periodically and is not a live service.

This edition includes grid connection approval (Aviz Tehnic de Racordare, ATR) capacity as listed by the Government of Romania; the ATR layer is described below with its limits.

In this demo

  • Power plants
  • Generators outside a plant
  • Substations
  • Power lines
  • County outlines
  • Country outline
  • Wind and cloud forecast (a dated weather forecast; not a forecast of generation, revenue or dispatch)
  • Solar irradiation, long-term (GHI) (long-term averages, not current site yield)
  • PV output potential, long-term (PVOUT) (long-term averages, not current site yield)
  • Daily national production by fuel (historical totals by day)
  • Listed ATR capacity (gov.ro list, April 2026) (county totals from one dated list)

Not in this demo

  • Grid development plan
  • Operational storage capacities
  • Production forecasts

How to read the evidence labels

  • OBSERVED: As published or as mapped in the source. Sol Associates has not checked these values against another source.
  • DERIVED: Computed from the source data by Sol Associates.
  • FORECAST: A model forecast, not a measurement.

  • native 0.25 deg cells and 3-hourly steps, no interpolation

Source: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (transparency.entsoe.eu), actual generation per production type, bidding zone RO. Daily totals computed by Sol Associates from the values published; not endorsed by ENTSO-E.
How it was made
Daily totals by fuel from ENTSO-E actual generation per production type (documentType A75, processType A16, domain RO) for the Europe/Bucharest local days 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-26. For each day that has a stored vintage, the energy of a fuel is the sum of mean power times interval length over the intervals that have a value (each fuel keeps its native interval and is never resampled); the totals are computed by Sol Associates and rounded to 0.1 MWh. A day without a stored vintage is empty (null), never zero. Interval series, excluded columns and raw files are not part of this layer.
Geography
Romania, ENTSO-E bidding zone RO (national total)
Coverage
7 of the last 7 complete local days (2026-10-20 to 2026-10-26, Europe/Bucharest)
What is included
Aggregated actual generation per production type as reported to ENTSO-E; storage charging load is excluded
Spatial resolution
National total, no spatial breakdown
Time resolution
One total per local calendar day and fuel; no per-interval values are published
What it does not show
  • National totals only: Romania, ENTSO-E bidding zone RO, one total per local calendar day (Europe/Bucharest) and fuel; no per-interval values and no per-plant allocation are published.
  • Each total is the sum over the day of mean power times interval length, computed by Sol Associates from the actual generation per production type that ENTSO-E published; it is not a daily figure published by ENTSO-E.
  • An interval for which ENTSO-E published no value is left out of the sum, so a day with gaps understates its energy; the table of the layer marks such days.
  • Generation only: pumped-storage and battery charging load is not netted, so hydro and battery totals are gross generation.
  • Values are as published when fetched and ENTSO-E may revise them later.
  • A value of 0 means ENTSO-E reported 0 MW for that fuel in every interval of that day that has a value; it is not a gap, and it does not by itself say why. A day without a value is shown as no data.

Listed ATR capacity (gov.ro list, April 2026)

DERIVEDunverified

What it shows
Reported ATR MW by county as listed by the source, with ATR date bands by the date recorded in the list (MW)
Kind of evidence
DERIVED. Computed from the source data by Sol Associates.
Verification
unverified. No independent check of these values has been made.
Date
Published 2026-04-25; the source gives no separate data date (185 days old at build; limit 400 days)
Source
https://gov.ro/ro/stiri/avize-tehnice-de-racordare-emise-pentru-80-000-mw-de-aproape-zece-ori-puterea-actuala-necesara-de-energie-a-romaniei
Licence
Reproduction authorised with the source named (gov.ro legal conditions); terms: https://gov.ro/ro/conditii-legale
Source: Guvernul României (gov.ro), Lista titularilor care dețin ATR, published 25.04.2026, reproduced with the source named; aggregated by Sol Associates SRL. The Government of Romania has not reviewed or endorsed this aggregation.
How it was made
Rows of the gov.ro list of ATR holders, published on 2026-04-25, are aggregated per county. Natural persons, rows that are not generation and corrupt or MW-less rows are excluded (their numbers are withheld in this edition); county names are matched to the OSM county outlines after folding case and diacritics and through a reviewed list of alternative spellings and codes; operators are normalised to the known grid operators; statuses are restricted to a closed vocabulary. Each ATR issue date is placed in a descriptive date band by the calendar dates in the band note. MW is rounded to 0.1 per cell and totals are sums of rounded cells. The retrieval time is taken as the last-modified time of the input file as held by Sol Associates; it is not the time the source was published.
Geography
Romania, county level (counties of the OSM outline layer, Bucuresti included) plus an unknown-county group (suppressed in this edition)
Coverage
gov.ro list of ATR holders: 1410 ATR rows aggregated (80227.7 MW) from the list published on 2026-04-25; excluded rows are not counted here (their number is withheld in this edition)
What is included
Listed ATR MW of the ATR holders on the gov.ro list, every stage as published, natural persons excluded
Spatial resolution
County; rows whose county cannot be matched form the unknown-county group, which is suppressed in this edition
Time resolution
One list, published on one date; the per-row ATR issue date is used only to assign date bands
What it does not show
  • Listed ATR MW in the source list for the selected source, status and technology: not installed capacity, not a built or connected plant, not a probability of commissioning, not MW per km2, saturation, headroom, grid loading or available capacity. Every stage is included (ATR issued, connection contract signed, works in execution, commissioned, other), so a total is not necessarily an outstanding queue.
  • Note that the issue date in the source may be the latest amended ATR date, not the date of the original ATR; here it is a date extracted from the ATR-number text (DERIVED; original or amendment unresolved): only a single date at the end of the text counts, and text with several dates, several ATR numbers or an amendment marker is ambiguous and the issue date unknown.
  • ATR date bands group source issue dates by the calendar dates in the band note; a band is never a legal assessment of any ATR. The band is set by the ATR issue date recorded in the source, which may be the date of the latest amended ATR. Under the cited acts the operative dates differ (for example the date a connection or capacity allocation request was registered, the date the operator communicated the financial guarantee value, or the date a connection contract was signed), so a band does not show which rules apply to a given ATR.
  • This list is one source: its figures must not be added to those of another source, because the same ATR can appear in more than one source.
  • MW is rounded to 0.1 per cell; county and total figures are sums of the rounded cells.
  • Operator names are normalised to the known grid operators (DEER means Distributie Energie Electrica Romania); any other value is shown as Other and a blank one as unknown.
  • Rows are matched to county names from OpenStreetMap-derived county boundaries after folding case and diacritics, then through a reviewed list of alternative spellings and codes; the number of rows matched that way is withheld in this edition. A county shown with zero MW is in the lowest class; a zero is not evidence that no ATR exists there.
  • Rows whose county is blank, abbreviated, names several counties or is misspelt, and cannot be matched, form an unknown-county group; it is suppressed in this edition and, in the totals, its rows and MW are counted as suppressed.
  • The band "Issue date unknown or ambiguous" holds the rows with no readable single issue date: a blank or free-text date, a text with several dates, several ATR numbers or an amendment marker (ambiguous), and a date after the day the list was published. The number of rows in each of these groups is withheld in this edition, so the band cannot be split further.
  • A status outside the closed vocabulary of the source is reported as Unrecognised with its MW kept and the raw value is not published; the number of such rows is withheld in this edition.
  • Because this list was published before 2026-05-25, the last date band is expected to be empty.
  • Status is Sol's English label for the stage column of the Government's list: 'PIF planificat' and 'Probe' are shown as 'Works in execution' and 'PIF realizat' as 'Commissioned'; where the list's detailed stage says the ATR is issued and a connection contract has not yet been requested, the row is shown as 'ATR issued'. Works in execution and commissioned are not independently observed progress or present operating status. These are the stage names of the Government list as read by Sol, not the stages of the ANRE connection procedure.
  • The list gives a technology for only some rows: rows without one are shown as technology Unknown, which does not mean the Unknown remainder is all generation or storage, and the share of Unknown technology is withheld in this edition.
  • Natural persons are excluded using the flag carried by the parsed list; the number excluded is withheld in this edition.
  • Date conflicts between sources are NOT ASSESSED for this layer (it compares no other source): the empty band "Issue date disagrees between sources" does not mean the sources agree.
  • The Government's list is a selection (holders with at least one megawatt in total) as published by the Government on 2026-04-25, and it may change; it covers every stage from newly issued ATRs to recent commissioning.
  • Rows are excluded when the source row is corrupt or its MW cannot be read; the number of such rows is withheld in this edition. Their capacity is unknown, not zero.
  • Counts and MW for particular groups of rows (excluded rows, rows matched through an alternative county spelling, rows with an unrecognised status, and the share of unknown technology) are not published in this edition, and neither is the split of the unknown-date band into its kinds (a blank or free-text date, an ambiguous text, a date after the day the list was published), so that the published figures are less likely to single out small groups.
  • The counts of rows with no readable issue date, of excluded rows and of rows without a matched county are not given in this edition: they are withheld, which is not the same as zero.
  • Detail is withheld or merged in this edition. Small groups are handled by threshold rules (at least 5 distinct holders per released figure, no holder above 80 percent of its MW), and figures that fail these tests are merged or withheld. These rules reduce, but cannot eliminate, the risk that a holder can be inferred when the figures are combined with the public Government list or other sources. Where the detail of a county could not be released it is merged into one remaining entry for that county, and where even that is too small the county is suppressed; national totals still include every aggregated row. The coverage figures show how much of the total MW is shown in detail, how much is merged and how much is suppressed. The same test is applied to every published figure and to the differences between published figures that isolate up to four groups of rows.

How to read the layers

Assets are as mapped in OpenStreetMap by volunteers; the map is not a census of the grid and how complete it is cannot be stated. Operator names are as tagged and may be wrong.

The weather layer is a model forecast, not a measurement, and not an energy yield, curtailment or grid loading estimate.

The solar layers are modelled long-term averages for 1994 to 2018, not forecasts and not a bankable yield.

The production layer shows national totals of electricity generation by fuel for the seven complete local days up to the build date, computed by Sol Associates from values published on the ENTSO-E Transparency Platform. It is not a measurement by Sol, not a forecast, and says nothing about prices, trading, curtailment or grid loading; a day with intervals that have no value understates its energy, and a day without data is empty, not zero.

The ATR layer shows connection-approval capacity (MW) listed by the Government in a list published on one date, summed by county. It is not installed capacity, grid loading, available or free capacity or a probability of commissioning, and it does not show whether any ATR is valid, eligible or subject to any rule.

Date bands group ATRs by the date recorded for them in the Government list, which can be the date of the latest amended ATR rather than of the original. They are descriptive. They do not show whether a project meets any rule or deadline, and Sol Associates draws no eligibility conclusion from them.
