Connection approvals (Government of Romania list)
  Source: Guvernul României (gov.ro), Lista titularilor care dețin ATR, published 25.04.2026.
  Reproduced with the source named, under the Government's legal conditions (https://gov.ro/ro/conditii-legale).
  The Government's list is a selection (holders of at least 1 MW in total) as published on that date; it may change, and it covers every stage from newly issued ATRs to recent commissioning.
  Aggregated by county by Sol Associates SRL. Rows identified as natural persons are excluded. Small groups are handled by threshold rules (at least 5 distinct holders per released figure, no holder above 80 percent of its MW), and figures that fail these tests are merged or withheld. These rules reduce, but cannot eliminate, the risk that a holder can be inferred when the figures are combined with the public Government list or other sources.
  The Government of Romania has not reviewed or endorsed this aggregation.
