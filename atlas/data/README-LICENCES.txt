The files assets_plants.json, assets_generators_unassigned.json, assets_substations.json, assets_lines_low.json, assets_lines_high.json, counties.json and country.json in this folder are a Derivative Database of OpenStreetMap as defined in the ODbL. You may copy, distribute, transmit and adapt them if you credit OpenStreetMap and its contributors, keep this notice, and offer any derivative database you publicly use under the same licence.
These files can be downloaded from this folder free of charge, in machine-readable form.

Changes made by Sol Associates: selection of power plants, generators, substations, lines, minor lines and cables, and of administrative boundaries (levels 2 and 4), inside Romania; polygons reduced to centroids; generators grouped into plants; county added from the boundaries; boundaries and lines simplified; voltage and output converted to kV and MW; coordinates rounded to 5 decimals; operator text withheld unless it is a company, public body or system operator.
The method of each layer is in manifest.json (transform_note).

OpenStreetMap is mapped by volunteers. These files are provided as is, without warranty (ODbL sections 7 and 8).

Other files in this folder are not part of this database and keep their own licences: weather_forecast.json (CC BY 4.0, ECMWF), solar_ghi.json and solar_pvout.json with their images (CC BY 4.0, Global Solar Atlas), production_national.json (daily totals computed by Sol Associates from ENTSO-E Transparency Platform data, see LICENSE-DATA.txt) and the ATR county file (county aggregates by Sol Associates SRL from the published list of the Government of Romania). See LICENSE-DATA.txt.
